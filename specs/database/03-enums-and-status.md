# DB-03 — Enums and Status

Trạng thái: `TARGET_V1_DRAFT`.

- `program_type`: `GOLDEN_HOUR`, `TWENTY_FOUR_SEVEN`; `24_7` is compatibility input only.
- `payment_method_snapshot`: `ONLINE`, `COD`; validated as GH+ONLINE or 24/7+COD.
- `execution_mode`: `MOCK`, `LAB_REAL_SIM`, `PRODUCTION_REAL`.
- job: `CREATED`, `ELIGIBILITY_RECHECK`, `QUEUED`, `CHANNEL_RESERVED`, `DIALING`, `RESULT`, `CALLBACK`, `CLOSED`, `HELD_ADMIN_REVIEW`, `BLOCKED`, `CAPACITY_HELD`, `DRY_RUN`.
- attempt: `SCHEDULED`, `DISPATCHING`, `RINGING`, `ANSWERED`, `CAPTURING_DTMF`, `COMPLETED`, `NO_ANSWER`, `INVALID_PHONE`, `TECHNICAL_EXCEPTION`, `WINDOW_EXPIRED`.
- result: `IVR_CONFIRMED`, `IVR_CUSTOMER_CANCELLED`, `IVR_NO_ANSWER_ATTEMPT`, `IVR_NO_ANSWER_FINAL`, `IVR_CONFIRMATION_WINDOW_EXPIRED`, `IVR_INVALID_PHONE_FINAL`, `IVR_WRONG_INPUT`, `IVR_TECHNICAL_EXCEPTION`, `IVR_CAPACITY_EXCEPTION`, `IVR_OPERATIONAL_BLOCKED`, `IVR_POLICY_BLOCKED`.
- callback delivery: `READY`, `SENDING`, `DELIVERED_ACCEPTED`, `DELIVERED_BLOCKED`, `DELIVERED_REVIEW`, `REJECTED_STALE`, `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`, `INVALID_DEAD_LETTER`, `RETRY_PENDING`, `RETRY_EXHAUSTED`.
- Sales ACK: `ACCEPTED`, `DUPLICATE_ACCEPTED`, `BLOCKED_BY_CORE`, `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `REJECTED_STALE`, `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`.
- SIM: `IDLE`, `RESERVED`, `ACTIVE_CALL`, `QUARANTINED`, `DISABLED`, `HEALTH_FAILED`.

Attempt policy timing/max are data/config tied to `attempt_policy_version`, not enums.
